The facility

Built for density, and for the neighbours

Structure · WJE

The steel frame has spare capacity at its original design loads. New roof dunnage carries the heat-rejection plant down to the columns, and targeted reinforcement brings the data-hall floors to high-density loading.

Acoustics · WorkingBuildings

An environmental noise model covers normal operation, generator testing and emergencies against Chicago and Illinois limits, with mitigation designed in.

Reuse

Keeping the frame and extending the existing chillers and towers protects the schedule and keeps embodied carbon well below a new build.

White space and the cluster

Halls laid out for liquid-cooled AI racks

The hall

  • Operable cabinets of about 130 kW, cooled liquid-to-chip with air for the remainder
  • Contained rows of twelve cabinets
  • About 80% of heat to the facility-glycol loop through CDUs and rear-door heat exchangers
  • Racks arrive fully built and roll in from the freight elevator
  • Structural layout checked by WJE for loaded racks of up to 4,500 lb

The ZODE cluster

  • Phase 1: 256 NVIDIA HGX B300 nodes, 2,048 GPUs, eight nodes to a 48U rack
  • Phases 2 and 3: NVIDIA Vera Rubin, 6,400 GPUs in 8-GPU node equivalents
  • Non-blocking InfiniBand compute fabric
  • Direct-to-chip liquid cooling throughout
